Shipping Policy

Last updated:

1. Dispatch Time

Orders are dispatched within 24–48 working hours of confirmation and payment (for prepaid orders).

2. Delivery Timeline

  • Metro cities: 2–4 working days
  • Tier-2/3 cities: 3–6 working days
  • Remote areas: 5–10 working days

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping is calculated at order confirmation based on weight, volume, and delivery pincode. Bulk orders may qualify for free shipping — contact your account manager.

4. Tracking

Tracking details are shared on WhatsApp / email after dispatch. You can also view status in My Orders.

5. Delivery Address

Please ensure the delivery address and phone number are correct. Re-delivery charges may apply for failed deliveries due to incorrect details.

6. Damage in Transit

Inspect the package on delivery. Report damage within 48 hours with photos via the Complaints portal.
