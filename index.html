Frequently Asked Questions

Everything distributors ask before applying.

Who can become an SVE distributor?

Any registered business — retailer, wholesaler, distributor, or super-stockist — with valid GST (or willingness to register) can apply. We also consider experienced traders without GST on a case-by-case basis.

Is there an application fee?

No. Applying is completely free. Once approved, you only pay for the stock you order.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

First order MOQ is ₹25,000. Subsequent orders can be smaller, based on your slab and territory.

How long does approval take?

Typically 24–48 working hours after all documents are submitted. You can track your application status anytime using your phone number or application ID.

Do you offer credit terms?

Yes. After the first 3 orders (prepaid), eligible distributors can apply for 15/30-day credit terms based on volume and payment history.

Which areas do you currently serve?

We dispatch pan-India via our logistics partners. Territory exclusivity depends on pincode availability — check during application.
