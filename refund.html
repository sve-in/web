Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated:

1. Return Window

Returns accepted within 48 hours of delivery for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. Report via the Complaints section.

2. Eligible Returns

  • Physically damaged goods (with photos of damage and packaging).
  • Wrong product shipped versus invoice.
  • Short quantity versus invoice.

3. Non-Eligible Returns

  • Opened, used, or repackaged products (unless defective).
  • Damage reported after 48 hours.
  • Custom or made-to-order items.

4. Process

  1. Raise a complaint on the portal with photos and order ID.
  2. Our team reviews and approves within 3 working days.
  3. For approved returns, we arrange pickup or ask you to ship back.
  4. Replacement or refund issued within 7-10 working days.

5. Refund Mode

Refunds are issued to the original payment method (bank transfer or credit adjustment to your account). Cash refunds are not provided.

Questions? Contact us.
